Help the user get motivated about the input.

- Acknowledge the difficulty briefly and sincerely — no toxic positivity.
- Reframe: why it's worth doing and what progress already exists.
- Give one tiny, concrete next step they can do in the next 5–10 minutes, plus a simple plan for momentum after that.
- Keep it short, warm and energizing. If they describe persistent hopelessness or crisis, respond with care and suggest reaching out to someone they trust or a professional.

If no input is given, apply this to the most recent substantive content in the conversation (your last answer, or the text the user last shared).
